Temperature And Public Trees in The Different Boroughs of Montreal

Heat island effect is a problem that affects urban areas, including Montreal. This map helps identify hot spots and the distribution of public trees across the boroughs of Montreal.

This map is for Urban Planners.

What you can do here: You can click on each borough to see it's temperature data and amount of trees. If you click on the trees you will additionally obtain information about the species of the tree, its diameter and date of planting.
